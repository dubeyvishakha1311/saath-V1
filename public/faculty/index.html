What this demonstration cannot establish

Trends, symptoms and responses were constructed by rules. Improvement, unchanged feelings and setbacks are deliberately mixed. No statistical significance, diagnosis or therapeutic benefit can be inferred. High-risk safety routing must be tested separately; this cohort excludes the Day 5 disappearance option.

The PDF is a standard English AcroForm worksheet, not an executable app. It has no authentication, backup encryption, scoring, media, reminders or automated safety detection. Save a copy and reopen it to verify responses persist before sharing securely.
